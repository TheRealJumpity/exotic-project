# Exotic Server Rules

Nov 24, 2026 · @Jumpity

## 1. General Provisions

1.1 These rules were drawn up by a representative of the Exotic project and apply to all game modes of the Exotic project. Ignorance of the rules does not release a violator from responsibility and may aggravate the punishment.

1.2 The Exotic project is a complex of servers located at the address: exotic.joinmc.world. All other domains are not part of the project. By registering on our project, you automatically accept these rules, as well as the rules of other game modes.

1.3 The rules may be changed without explicit notice to the project's main audience. In the event of significant changes to the rules, an announcement is posted on the project forum.

1.4 The decisions and actions of the head administration (\[TheRealJumpity\], \[HomHom2011\], \[Exotic Team\]) are not open to discussion and cannot be appealed. The head administration has the right to issue punishments outside the scope of these rules.

1.5 Server administration does not issue game resources or compensation for losses resulting from a server kick, lag/bugs, or the actions of players using prohibited software (cheats).

1.6 Selling, hacking, attempted hacking, transferring accounts, buying accounts outside the official website, distributing player data, causing harm to the server, as well as selling/buying anything for real currency, are strictly prohibited. Punishment: all accounts are disabled with no option to purchase an unban. Complicity, assistance, and playing together with violators also fall under this rule. Accounts that have caused serious harm to the project cannot be restored, even if the account was hacked, voluntarily transferred, or sold at the time. The account owner bears responsibility, regardless of who performed the actions under that account.

## 2. Registration Rules

2.1 A user's nickname must be appropriate. An inappropriate nickname is any nickname that violates the established rules, as well as the following: \[account ban; for changed nicknames – a warning, then a ban\]

- 2.1.1 A nickname similar to the nickname of the senior Administration (exception: nicknames of the project's YouTubers).
- 2.1.2 A nickname of a sexual or immoral nature (names of genitalia, sexual acts, etc.).
- 2.1.3 A nickname that insults another player's nickname.
- 2.1.4 A nickname containing the names of prohibited software (example: Celestial777, Meteor_Destroyer,).
- 2.1.5 A nickname containing profanity.
- 2.1.6 A nickname containing words added to the Anti-Spam system, even if disguised (for example: advertising YouTubers, third-party projects).
- 2.1.7 A nickname related to the death of a project staff member.

2.2 When purchasing paid extras (ranks), cases, or premium currency, you must save the receipt provided by the system.

2.3 If you lose your account or it is hacked, you must contact technical support. If your account was linked, you can restore it yourself. Without an electronic receipt, restoration will be refused. For security reasons, the project does not resend receipts. Link to the project's technical support: \[Technical Support Link\]

- 2.3.1 The project does not guarantee account security; the procedure for securing the account rests entirely with the player. If an account is lost, the administration has the right to refuse restoration, and may also fully reset and ban the account if it caused serious harm to the project and its intellectual property rights.

## 3. Paid Services

3.1 Playing on the project does not require payment for the use of Exotic resources.

3.2 A player has the right to voluntarily pay a certain amount to the project for an in-game service, on a one-sided basis.

3.3 If a payment fails, or an in-game service is missing after payment, you must contact technical support with the electronic receipt sent to the email you provided. Without an electronic receipt, restoration of the service will be refused. For security reasons, the project does not resend receipts. The receipt must be saved at the time of payment or found in your email.

3.4 We do not offer refunds for purchases, because Exotic is not a commercial organization and all purchases are voluntary donations toward the development of the server.

3.6 Purchasing paid ranks does not grant any privilege to ignore or violate these rules.

## 4. In-Game Communication

4.1 Flooding is prohibited: sending more than 2 messages that are similar in meaning or identical in a row (2 messages are allowed, 3 are not) within one minute. \[4-hour mute\]

- 4.1.1 It is prohibited to coordinate with other players or use your alt accounts to send the same message within a short period of time, in other words, to bypass the flood punishment using third-party accounts. \[4-hour mute for each participant\]
- 4.1.1.2 If mass flooding was organized by sending identical messages within a short period of time, the participants are punished (for example, mass sending of "F" after a player drops an expensive rank from a case). \[4-hour mute for each participant\]
- 4.1.2 It is prohibited to use announcements to bypass the flood punishment (using 2 /bc and a message in global chat, or vice versa, 2 messages and 1 /bc). \[6-hour mute\]

4.2 It is prohibited to flood with symbols, i.e. to use combinations of meaningless symbols, such as "!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!", or laughter spam. \[4-hour mute, more than 10 characters\]

- 4.2.1 Abuse of caps: if more than 50% of a sentence (of at least 3 words) is written in caps. \[4-hour mute\]

4.3 It is prohibited to organize flooding through polls, such as "send + in chat". Exceptions: requests for shelter, or to sell or trade resources. Messages of the following types are also exceptions: "who will accept me into a clan + in chat / who wants a duel + in chat / who wants to survive together + in chat". \[4-hour mute\]

- 4.3.1 If 6 or more people respond to your message while organizing a flood, you receive a 6-hour mute.

4.4 Inappropriate behavior, excessive insults, and aggression. \[12-hour mute\]

## 7. Moderator Rights During Checks

7.1 Moderation has the right to call you via AnyDesk; checks are conducted only according to the regulations at \[AnyDesk Regulations Link\].

- 7.1.1 Moderation has no right to ask for full access in AnyDesk. Only mouse and keyboard access.

7.2 Checks are conducted exclusively on a computer, never on phones. Moderation has no right to send you programs from their computer. All programs for checking for cheats are downloaded by you in a browser.

Mandatory: SystemInformer, Everything, RecentFilesView, ShellbagAnalyzer, RegScanner, JournalTrace.

Optional, allowed for use during checks: Recaf, JD-gui, JumplistsView, BrowsersDownloadsView, WinPrefetchView, Recuva.

Download only from official websites or GitHub (first link).4 

7.3 Moderators with increased trust (moder+) and above have the right to check your FunPay and other sites where resources can be listed for real currency.

7.4 Checks are conducted only via AnyDesk. Programs for checks must be downloaded only from the official website; moderation has no right to send you a file and ask you to download it. If a check via AnyDesk cannot be conducted due to connection problems, you may be checked via Discord.
