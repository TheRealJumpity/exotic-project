HOW TO JOIN

Three steps from the title screen to the server.

01

OPEN MULTIPLAYER

Launch Minecraft and click Multiplayer on the title screen.

02

ADD THE SERVER

Choose Add Server, name it Exotic, and enter the address exotic.joinmc.world.

03

JOIN & PLAY FAIR

Hit Join Server. Registering means you accept the server rules, so give them a read first.
